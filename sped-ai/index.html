What this trains

A safer way to use AI in high-stakes educator workflows

  • Separate verified evidence from AI inference.
  • Apply privacy and approved-system gates before prompting.
  • Bound AI to assistive tasks, not professional decisions.
  • Detect unsupported facts, strengthened claims, and hidden guesses.
  • Keep eligibility, services, accommodations, goals, and IEP-team decisions with qualified humans.

The operating rule

AI may assist the work. It does not become the source of truth.

The useful boundary is not “AI allowed” versus “AI banned.” It is a sequence of controls.

Source → Privacy Gate → Bound → Generate → Verify → Approve
Evidence stays authoritativeAI may transform verified information; it may not invent student facts.
Privacy comes firstDistrict approval and data rules are checked before usefulness.
