# Diligence Lab — Initial Market Test

**Status:** outreach preparation, not evidence of client work or commercial traction.

The objective of this market test is narrow: determine whether established M&A / private-equity diligence teams see value in adding a specialist reproducibility layer for technically material AI claims.

## Offer being tested

**Technical Claims Diligence — Pilot Module**

One material claim → frozen evidence chain → reproduction → 3–5 targeted controls → discrepancy localization → decision memo + evidence appendix.

The module is designed to complement an existing diligence team, not compete with full transaction advisory.

## Qualification rule for additional targets

Do not spray generic PE firms.

A target enters the first wave only if at least one of these is true:

1. it already sells technology or AI diligence to PE clients;
2. it has a software/product technical-diligence practice;
3. it evaluates AI-enabled acquisitions where benchmark or model-performance claims can affect the investment thesis;
4. it has a plausible subcontractor/specialist model.

## First-wave experiment

Start with **5 highly matched conversations**, not 100 cold emails.

Measure:

- reply / referral to correct person;
- request to see examples;
- willingness to discuss a pilot;
- objections to scope or credibility;
- whether the buyer values independent reproduction specifically;
- whether the need appears only during live deals.

The market test falsifies the commercial hypothesis if well-targeted diligence leaders consistently understand the offer but do not see independent technical-claim reproduction as useful enough to insert into a live diligence workflow.

## What not to claim

Until earned, do not claim:

- prior PE client engagements;
- transaction advisory experience;
- proprietary diligence technology;
- full-company technical certification;
- that the lab can determine valuation by itself;
- external validation of the commercial service.

The present proof is the public research methodology and cases. The next proof must be a real buyer conversation, pilot, or paid engagement.
