# Diligence Lab — First-Pilot Outreach Kit

**Status:** internal outreach asset; no client relationship or engagement is implied.

## Objective

Secure one narrow live pilot in which an existing private-equity, M&A, or technology-diligence team has a material AI/ML performance claim that would benefit from independent reproducibility and adversarial testing.

The pitch is **not** "hire me to replace your diligence team." It is:

> I built a narrow reproducibility-first module that can sit underneath an existing technology-diligence workstream when a target's AI/ML performance claim materially affects the investment thesis.

Public pilot scope: https://robert-morong-research.netlify.app/diligence.html

Public evidence: https://github.com/GrobeStreet/robert-morong-research/tree/main/diligence

## Outreach discipline

1. Do not mass-email PE firms.
2. Start with practitioners who already own technology/AI diligence workflows.
3. Link to evidence; do not attach a deck on first contact.
4. Do not use "elite," "expert," "proprietary methodology," or other unsupported status language.
5. Do not quote a large retainer before a real use case exists.
6. Ask for one narrow pilot, not a role or broad partnership.
7. If the first response is "interesting but not our workflow," ask what evidence/output would make it usable before building more artifacts.
8. Treat rejection as product discovery: record why the module did not map to the real diligence process.

## What not to promise

- complete company diligence;
- security certification;
- legal/compliance diligence;
- guaranteed fraud detection;
- benchmark decontamination where training data are unknowable;
- causal attribution without an identification strategy;
- independent verification when the work is only regenerated internally;
- conclusions beyond the access and evidence actually provided.

## Pilot commercial posture

Do not optimize price before validating demand. For the first real engagement, scope around a single claim and a bounded deliverable. The initial commercial objective is a **paid proof that the module reduces decision uncertainty**, followed by evidence about turnaround time, access requirements, buyer value, and repeatability.
