Cosmology · DESI DR2

Where does the evolving-dark-energy preference live?

The published compressed-likelihood preference reproduces, but much of its strength is LRG2-sensitive.

  • Direct constant-w vs CPL calibration: 2.75σ.
  • Without LRG2: 1.81σ.
  • Conditional selected-influence p-value: 0.486.

LRG2 is load-bearing descriptively, but its selection as the most influential tracer is not additional independent evidence once the global fluctuation is conditioned on.

AI evaluation · MMLU

Does a model keep its answer when options move?

The regenerated Qwen harness changes its underlying answer on roughly 78% of sampled questions under cyclic option reordering in both bf16 and fp32.

  • Flipping-question accuracy remains near chance.
  • Random tie-breaking does not remove the effect.
  • fp32 eliminates exact ties but leaves the effect essentially unchanged.

Historical stability and calibration quantities did not regenerate and were explicitly downgraded rather than defended.

Receipts: green CI · regeneration report · frozen provenance · historical vs regenerated tables · failed explanations retained.

AI evaluation · ARC-AGI-2

How much does demonstration consistency really tell us?

Corrected same-holdout reliability is 32.8% / 50.8% / 63.4% at k=1/2/3. MDL selection gains +11.1 points over random on ambiguous cases.

  • Candidate oracle is only +3.7 points above MDL.
  • The linked solver remains 0/167.
  • The corrected design overturned an earlier optimistic result.

The public record preserves the self-correction and publish-regardless commitment instead of promoting the earlier result.
