AI evaluation · MMLU

Option-order robustness

The regenerated Qwen harness changes its underlying answer on roughly 78% of questions under both bf16 and fp32, while accuracy on flipping questions remains near chance.

Headline accuracy and the majority-flip result regenerated, but the historical stability, ECE, and mean four-label confidence values did not. Tie-breaking and dtype were tested and do not explain the gap; the historical Qwen-vs-Llama calibration contrast remains unconfirmed until the Llama arm is rerun.
