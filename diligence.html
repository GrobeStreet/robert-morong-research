The wedge

One claim. One decision. Inspectable evidence.

Best-fit claims

Claims that materially influence technical moat, valuation, integration risk, or post-close value creation: benchmark superiority, proprietary model/scaffold lift, accuracy, cost reduction, robustness, fine-tuning attribution, or production-transfer claims.

Not a generic diligence report

This module complements broader M&A, security, commercial, architecture, and management diligence. It does not certify an entire company. It makes one important technical claim more falsifiable and decision-useful.

Workflow

Five stages

1 · Freeze

Record exact wording, metric, source, model/configuration, benchmark version, comparator, date, and decision relevance.

2 · Reproduce

Attempt the advertised result without silently changing the protocol. Preserve commands, versions, logs, outputs, and provenance.

3 · Stress-test

Run 3–5 controlled tests against the most plausible fragilities: seed, prompt/order, holdout shift, scaffold attribution, budget, calibration, contamination, or model version.

4 · Localize

Identify where disagreement first appears: provenance, environment, data, harness, stochasticity, comparator, attribution, or underlying performance.

5 · Translate

State what reproduced, what did not, what remains unresolved, how much decision weight the claim deserves, and the cheapest decisive follow-up test.

Evidence states

Reported ≠ reconstructed ≠ regenerated ≠ independently verified. Unresolved evidence stays unresolved.

Deliverables

Built for an investment or operating team

  • 2-page decision memo, conclusion first
  • frozen claim ledger
  • reproduction record
  • 3–5 test stress matrix
  • failure anatomy
  • evidence appendix with code/version/command/log references
  • single highest-value remaining test

Success criterion: the work must reduce material decision uncertainty. Producing a longer technical report is not itself success.
